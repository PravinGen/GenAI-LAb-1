However, based on common recommendations, university syllabi, and general consensus within the electrical engineering community, here are some of the **most influential and widely recognized electrical engineering books**, presented with the details you requested. I've tried to select books that are foundational and consistently recommended across different sub-disciplines.

**Please note:** For some older, classic books, the ISBN might be harder to find for the *absolute* first edition. I'm providing ISBNs for readily available and commonly used editions.

---

### **Foundational & General Electrical Engineering**

These books cover broad aspects of electrical engineering and are often used as introductory texts.

*   **Title:** Fundamentals of Electric Circuits
    *   **ISBN (13-digit):** 978-1119000071
    *   **Published Year:** 2017 (for the 7th edition, but earlier editions are also very popular)
    *   **Author:** Charles K. Alexander, Matthew N. O. Sadiku

*   **Title:** Electric Circuits
    *   **ISBN (13-digit):** 978-1119489073
    *   **Published Year:** 2018 (for the 11th edition, a very well-regarded classic)
    *   **Author:** James W. Nilsson, Susan A. Riedel

---

### **Signals and Systems**

Essential for understanding communication systems, control systems, and signal processing.

*   **Title:** Signals and Systems
    *   **ISBN (13-digit):** 978-0138147570
    *   **Published Year:** 2010 (for the 2nd edition, a very influential text)
    *   **Author:** Alan V. Oppenheim, Alan S. Willsky, S. Hamid Nawab

---

### **Electromagnetics**

Crucial for understanding antennas, RF engineering, and wave propagation.

*   **Title:** Elements of Electromagnetics
    *   **ISBN (13-digit):** 978-0190227139
    *   **Published Year:** 2016 (for the 7th edition, a comprehensive and popular choice)
    *   **Author:** Matthew N. O. Sadiku

*   **Title:** Engineering Electromagnetics
    *   **ISBN (13-digit):** 978-0199339034
    *   **Published Year:** 2015 (for the 8th edition, another highly respected classic)
    *   **Author:** William H. Hayt Jr., John A. Buck

---

### **Semiconductor Devices & Electronics**

Fundamental for understanding integrated circuits and modern electronics.

*   **Title:** Microelectronic Circuits
    *   **ISBN (13-digit):** 978-0190200028
    *   **Published Year:** 2018 (for the 8th edition, a cornerstone for analog and digital circuit design)
    *   **Author:** Adel S. Sedra, Kenneth C. Smith, Tony R. Sedra

---

### **Digital Signal Processing**

Key for audio, image, and communication signal manipulation.

*   **Title:** Digital Signal Processing: Principles, Algorithms, and Applications
    *   **ISBN (13-digit):** 978-0470237437
    *   **Published Year:** 2013 (for the 4th edition, a comprehensive and widely cited text)
    *   **Author:** John G. Proakis, Dimitris G. Manolakis

---

### **Control Systems**

Essential for automation, robotics, and system stability.

*   **Title:** Modern Control Engineering
    *   **ISBN (13-digit):** 978-0136019695
    *   **Published Year:** 2010 (for the 5th edition, a very popular and thorough treatment)
    *   **Author:** Katsuhiko Ogata

**How to find more "popular" books:**

*   **Check University Syllabi:** Many university electrical engineering departments publish their course syllabi online. Look for required or recommended texts in core courses.
*   **Browse Online Retailers:** On Amazon, Barnes & Noble, or other book retailers, look at the "Bestsellers" or "Most Wished For" lists within the Electrical Engineering category. Also, check customer reviews and ratings.
*   **Consult Professional Organizations:** IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) and other professional bodies often have recommended reading lists or publications.
*   **Look at Citation Counts:** For academic research, checking how often a book is cited in other papers (e.g., using Google Scholar or IEEE Xplore) can indicate its influence.
